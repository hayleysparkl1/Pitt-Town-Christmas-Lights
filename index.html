Pitt Town Christmas Lights Trail

A community project for Pitt Town, NSW

Christmas lights bring us together.

Find the decorated homes around Pitt Town and plan a festive evening with family and friends.

The trail is being prepared for this Christmas.

How it will work

1

Share a decorated home

Residents will be able to submit their address if they’d like their Christmas lights included.

2

Choose your stops

Visitors will be able to pick the homes they want to visit and arrange them into a trail.

3

Open your trail in Maps

The finished trail will be easy to open in Google Maps for directions around town.

Made by the community

This is a community project to help neighbours share the Christmas spirit. The trail will only include homes whose residents choose to take part.
